Google Play & Apple App Store Compliance

Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: September 24, 2026

At GymOS, we deeply respect your right to privacy and data sovereignty. In accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies on User Data & Account Deletion and Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), this page provides transparent instructions on how you can request the permanent deletion of your GymOS account and all associated personal data across both Android and iOS devices.

Option 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

You can permanently delete your account directly from within the GymOS mobile app without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Open the GymOS app on your device.
  2. Log in to your account if you haven't already.
  3. Navigate to the More / Settings tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Tap on Settings & Privacy.
  5. Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap the red "Delete Account" button.
  6. Read the confirmation dialog outlining what data will be erased, and confirm by tapping "Confirm Delete".

Note: Once confirmed, your session is immediately terminated, and your account credentials are permanently disabled.

Option 2: Web-Based Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the GymOS mobile app, you can submit an official account deletion request via email or by calling our support helpline:

  • Email: Send an email from your registered email address to support@gymos.tech.
  • Phone / WhatsApp Helpline: Call or message us at +91 8160490013.
  • Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request
  • Required Information:
    • Your registered Full Name
    • Your registered Email Address or Phone Number
    • Your Role (Gym Owner, Trainer/Staff, or Member)
    • Registered Gym Name (if applicable)
✉ Submit Deletion Request via Email 📞 Call Support: +91 8160490013

Our security team verifies ownership and processes all web requests within 48 to 72 business hours.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

Upon execution of an account deletion request, the following records are permanently erased from our primary databases (Supabase / PostgreSQL cloud storage):

  • Authentication & Profile Data: Name, email address, password hashes, OAuth tokens, phone number, and uploaded profile avatars.
  • Gym Owner Data (if applicable): Gym profile, staff registries, member lists, custom membership plans, and workout/diet assignments.
  • Member & Staff Records: Attendance logs, check-in history, salary records, commission calculations, and fitness progress tracking.
  • Device & Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging device push tokens.

Data Retention Period & Legal Exceptions

In accordance with data protection regulations and applicable tax and commercial laws:

  • Active Production Purge: Primary databases purge your identity and profile records immediately or within a maximum of 30 days.
  • Disaster Recovery Backups: Secure cryptographic backup snapshots are purged on our regular 90-day rolling cycle.
  • Statutory Financial Records: Any payment transaction IDs, invoices, or billing records processed via Razorpay are retained for the minimum statutory period strictly required by law for taxation and auditing purposes. These records cannot be used for any marketing or commercial purpose.
